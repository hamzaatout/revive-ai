Project Abstract

The computational restoration and colorization of historical archival photographs requires a specialized dual-pipeline artificial intelligence architecture. The primary pipeline executes structural restoration, involving the semantic inpainting of physical damage, denoising of organic film grain, and deblurring of optical aberrations resulting from archaic lenses. The secondary pipeline executes semantic image colorization, an inherently ill-posed problem that leverages deep semantic priors learned from vast visual datasets to hallucinate a perceptually vibrant and contextually accurate color palette.

Phase 1: Structural Restoration

Architectures

Overcoming the domain gap between mathematically synthesized noise and real physical film damage requires specialized topologies.

Bringing Old Photos Back to Life

Addresses the failure of direct pixel-to-pixel mapping using a triplet domain translation problem. Utilizes dual Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) to project disparate inputs into a shared latent space, closing the domain gap before restorative mapping.

SwinIR & Restormer

Uses Vision Transformers (ViTs) and Residual Swin Transformer Blocks (RSTB) to model long-range global dependencies efficiently. Shifts window partitioning to reduce computational complexity from quadratic to linear.

GFP-GAN

Resolves "blind face restoration" by exploiting Generative Facial Priors from StyleGAN2. Modulates synthesized features via Channel-Split Spatial Feature Transform (CS-SFT) layers to preserve specific biometrics.

DiffBIR (Latent Diffusion)

A rigorous two-stage pipeline. Stage 1 strips noise/blur using SwinIR, while Stage 2 employs IRControlNet (Stable Diffusion backbone) to iteratively inject photorealistic, high-frequency textural details back into the image.

Datasets

Training robust models requires enormous datasets of pristine high-resolution imagery juxtaposed with highly curated authentic physical damage.

Pristine Datasets (Synthetic Degradation)

  • FFHQ (Flickr-Faces-HQ): 70k diverse high-fidelity facial images at 1024x1024 resolution. Critical for StyleGAN priors.
    github.com/NVlabs/ffhq-dataset
  • CelebAMask-HQ: 30k images with pixel-perfect segmentation masks for 19 distinct facial attributes.
    github.com/switchablenorms/CelebAMask-HQ
  • Places2: Over 10 million images spanning 400 scene categories to prevent background degradation failures.
    places2.csail.mit.edu

Real & Paired Scratch Datasets

  • ScraOld: 703 meticulously curated pairs of authentic scratched photographs and their manually restored ground-truths.
  • OSPD (Old Scratched Photo Dataset): 238 highly complex scratch/scratch-free pairs for training contextual assistance mechanisms.
    github.com/cwyyt/...

Phase 2: Image Colorization

Architectures

Translating single-channel grayscale into three-channel RGB requires predicting an infinite mathematical distribution of potential chrominance values.

Pix2Pix (Conditional GANs)

Utilizes a deep U-Net backbone generator and a PatchGAN discriminator that assesses realism at localized N×N patches to force high-frequency chromatic textures.

DeOldify

Solves spatial inconsistency and temporal flickering via a novel "NoGAN" training methodology. Pre-trains generator and critic independently, applying only a brief adversarial shock to inject vibrant color without accruing typical GAN artifacts.

DDColor (Dual Decoders)

Solves "color bleeding" by decoupling structural reconstruction from semantic color processing. Uses a Pixel Decoder for structure and a query-based Color Decoder, linking them via rigid cross-attention.

Palette (Latent Diffusion)

Adapts text-to-image latent diffusion into conditional translation. Reverses denoising based strictly on the grayscale condition, yielding photorealistic contextual colors utilizing deep semantic understanding.

Datasets

Colorization networks must ingest massive datasets of modern color imagery to learn mathematical input-output mappings.

Standard Broad-Domain Datasets

  • ImageNet: 14 million annotated images. Used heavily by DeOldify for its brief NoGAN adversarial phase.
    image-net.org
  • COCO (Common Objects in Context): 330k "scene-centric" images with complex environments to resolve object-centric bias.
    docs.ultralytics.com/coco

Domain-Specific Historical Datasets

  • Historical Color Image Dataset (HCID): 1,325 authentic color photographs (1930s-1970s). Captures exact chemical color profiles to prevent modern "HDR-style" anachronisms.
    graphics.cs.cmu.edu
  • ViCoW (Vietnam War Imagery): 1,896 high-res image pairs extracted from historical films, serving as a benchmark for severe temporal degradation and archival media adaptation.

Evaluation Metrics & Baselines

Mathematical Evaluation Metrics

Evaluating generative AI models requires a departure from legacy mathematical assessments toward deep perceptual metrics.

PSNR & SSIM

Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) calculates raw spatial pixel errors using Mean Squared Error. Structural Similarity Index (SSIM) improves upon this by evaluating luminance, contrast, and structure.

MSE = (1/mn) * Σ[I(i,j) - K(i,j)]²

Limitation: Heavily penalizes generative hallucination despite visual realism.

LPIPS (Learned Perceptual Image Patch Similarity)

The gold standard for generative models. Abandons raw pixels for deep-learning-based perceptual similarity using internal feature activations of pre-trained networks (VGGNet/AlexNet).

d₀(x, x₀) = Σ ||wˡ ⊙ (yˡ - y₀ˡ)||₂²

FID (Fréchet Inception Distance)

A non-reference, distribution-based metric that computes the Wasserstein-2 distance between the statistical distribution of real photographs and generated images using Inception-v3 features.

FID = ||μ_r - μ_g||₂² + Tr(Σ_r + Σ_g - 2√(Σ_r Σ_g))
